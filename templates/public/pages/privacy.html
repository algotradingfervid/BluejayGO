Privacy Notice

This notice describes the information collected when you send an enquiry through this website.

Information in your enquiry

The contact form collects your name, email address, phone number, company, inquiry type and message. Your message may include the product you are asking about. The submission also records your IP address and browser information.

How enquiry details are used

Your enquiry is stored for the BlueJay team to review and respond to. Contact details help the team follow up on your request.

What to include

Include only information needed for your enquiry. Do not send passwords, payment card details or sensitive personal information through the message field.

Questions about your information

Use the contact details on our Contact Us page to ask about information you submitted or to request a correction. Identify the enquiry without including unnecessary personal information.
